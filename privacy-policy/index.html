Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Welcome to Ai Fitness Alarm. We are committed to protecting your privacy while helping you achieve your fitness goals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect and Why

To provide a seamless "fitness-first" alarm experience, we require specific permissions on your device. We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the app's functionality.

Core App Functionalities

Camera Used solely for AI-driven fitness verification (e.g., ensuring you are out of bed). Images are processed locally and are never stored on our servers.
Activity Recognition Used for step counting to detect movement and verify that you have started your morning activity.
Alarms & System Tools Utilizes SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WAKE_LOCK, and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to ensure alarms trigger reliably.

Connectivity & Analytics

  • Internet & Network State: Used to check for app updates, sync settings, and provide AI model improvements.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used for analytics and to provide relevant, non-intrusive advertisements.

User Experience

  • Foreground Services: Used to play alarm audio and maintain fitness tracking sessions.
  • Notifications & Vibration: Used to alert you of upcoming alarms and provide haptic feedback.

2. Data Usage and Security

We value your trust. Here is how we handle your data:

  • No Personal Sale: We do not sell your personal data or fitness activity to third parties.
  • On-Device Processing: AI analysis happens directly on your smartphone to maximize privacy.
  • Encryption: Data transmitted to our servers is protected via industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses trusted third-party tools that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • AdMob
  • Unity Ads

4. Your Choices and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Permission Management: You can revoke permissions (Camera, Activity, etc.) via Android System Settings.
  • Opt-out of Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of interest-based ads in your Google Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If discovered, such data is immediately deleted.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
