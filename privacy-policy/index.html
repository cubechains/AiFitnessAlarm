Privacy Policy Effective Date: April 1, 2026 Welcome to Ai Fitness Alarm. We are committed to protecting your privacy while helping you achieve your fitness goals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI Fitness Alarm application. 1. Information We Collect and Why To provide a seamless "fitness-first" alarm experience, we require specific permissions on your device. We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the app's functionality. Core App Functionalities Camera: Used solely for AI-driven fitness verification (e.g., ensuring you are out of bed or performing a specific exercise to silence the alarm). Images are processed locally on-device and are not stored on our servers. Activity Recognition (Step Counting): Used to detect movement and verify that you have started your morning activity. Alarms & System Tools: We use SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, WAKE_LOCK, and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to ensure your fitness alarms trigger reliably, even after a device reboot. Connectivity & Analytics Internet & Network State: Used to check for app updates, sync settings (if applicable), and provide AI model improvements. Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used for analytics and to provide relevant, non-intrusive advertisements that help keep the app free to use. User Experience Foreground Services: We use FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK to play your chosen alarm audio and maintain the fitness tracking session while the app is in use. Notifications & Vibration: Used to alert you of upcoming alarms and provide haptic feedback during fitness challenges. 2. Data Usage and Security We value your trust. Here is how we handle your data: No Personal Sale: We do not sell your personal data or fitness activity to third parties. On-Device Processing: Whenever possible, AI analysis (like exercise detection) happens directly on your smartphone to maximize privacy. Encryption: Any data transmitted to our servers (such as crash reports or anonymous analytics) is encrypted via Industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. 3. Third-Party Services Our app may use third-party tools (like Google Play Services) that collect information used to identify you. These services may include: Google Analytics for Firebase (To understand app performance) AdMob (To display advertisements) Unity ads (To display advertisements) Each of these providers has their own privacy policy regarding how they handle the AD_ID and usage data. 4. Your Choices and Controls You have full control over the data you share: Permission Management: You can revoke any permission (Camera, Activity Recognition, etc.) at any time through your Android System Settings. Note that this may disable core features of the alarm. Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized interest-based ads in your device’s Google Settings. 5. Children's Privacy Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. 6. Changes to This Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. 7.
